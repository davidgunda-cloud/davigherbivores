Lab results (nutrition)

Give each sampled plant a lab sample ID on the Record tab. When results arrive, import them as a CSV with a sample_id column and any of CP, NDF, ADF, ADL, CT, TP, Ash, DM (% of dry matter). The template lists your sample IDs.

Phenology from satellite

Monthly greenness (Sentinel-2 NDVI) for the study site, without a field visit. Higher NDVI means more green leaves; around 0.2 is dry and leafless.

Update satellite data

Run the phenology script in Google Earth Engine, export Makuyuni_monthly_NDVI_per_point.csv to Drive, then import it here. It replaces the data for the site selected above.
